Edge AI

INTELLIGENCE,
WHERE DATA HAPPENS.

Edge AI brings machine intelligence closer to the devices creating the data — enabling responsive, resilient and privacy-aware intelligent experiences without requiring every inference request to travel to the cloud.

Traditional AI can send data to remote infrastructure for processing. Edge AI moves appropriate inference closer to the source of the data.

Sometimes the best architecture is both.

Why Edge AI

Designed for the Edge

⚡

SPEED

Processing closer to the source can reduce dependence on remote round trips, enabling more responsive experiences.

🔒

PRIVACY

Some sensitive data can be processed locally rather than continuously transmitted.

🛡️

RESILIENCE

Local inference can remain useful when connectivity is limited.

📊

EFFICIENCY

Processing locally can reduce unnecessary data movement in appropriate workloads.

Applications

EDGE AI IN THE REAL WORLD

Explore how edge AI could be applied across domains. Click a card to learn more.

Smart Cameras ▾Generate video frames. Local AI can detect objects or anomalies in real time. Local inference reduces bandwidth needs and enables immediate alerts.
Robotics ▾Generate sensor and motor data. Local AI can enable real-time motion control and object avoidance. Low latency is critical for safe operation.
Drones ▾Generate video and flight telemetry. Local AI can support navigation and inspection tasks, even when connectivity is intermittent.
Vehicles ▾Generate camera, radar, and vehicle data. Local AI can support driver assistance and autonomous features that require immediate response.
Industrial IoT ▾Generate sensor and machine telemetry. Local AI can detect anomalies and predict maintenance needs on the factory floor.
Healthcare Wearables ▾Generate heart-rate, movement, and oxygen data. Local AI can monitor vital signs and flag concerning patterns while keeping data on-device.
Agriculture ▾Generate field imagery and soil sensor data. Local AI can identify crop health issues and optimize resource use in remote locations.
Smartphones ▾Generate camera, audio, and touch data. Local AI can power real-time language translation, face recognition, and camera enhancements.
Energy Systems ▾Generate grid and sensor data. Local AI can help balance loads and detect equipment issues in distributed infrastructure.
Smart Homes ▾Generate motion, audio, and environmental data. Local AI can support responsive automation while keeping household data on-site.

EDGE AI SECURITY

Edge AI does not automatically solve security or privacy challenges.

Secure updatesSigned model and firmware updates prevent tampering during deployment.
Model protectionEncryption and obfuscation can deter reverse engineering, but no method is absolute.
Device compromisePhysical access to edge hardware introduces unique attack surfaces.
Encrypted communicationWhen data does travel, encryption protects it in transit.
Access controlRole-based permissions govern device and data interactions.
Adversarial inputsModels can be fooled by carefully crafted inputs; robustness is an active research area.
Privacy risksLocal processing reduces some risks but does not eliminate all privacy concerns.
